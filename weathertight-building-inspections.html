Overview

Weathertight Building Inspections done properly

A weathertightness inspection tells you whether water is getting into the building envelope, where it is coming from and how serious it is. Our qualified inspector combines calibrated non invasive moisture meters with full thermal imaging to scan the exterior and interior, element by element and room by room.

If elevated moisture readings or thermal anomalies are found, we explain exactly what they mean and recommend the right next step, whether that is invasive testing, targeted repairs or simple preventative maintenance.

What is included

  • Non invasive moisture testing
  • Full thermal imaging scan
  • Exterior cladding and junction review
  • Detailed photo report
  • Clear recommendations
  • Report to NZS 4306:2005

What is a weathertight building inspection?+
It is a specialist inspection that checks whether water is entering the building envelope. We use non invasive moisture testing and full thermal imaging to find hidden moisture, then explain what it means and what to do next.
How long does it take to get my report?+
Reports are detailed yet easy to read, with photos and comments throughout. We will confirm the turnaround when you book so it fits your settlement or sale timeline.
Do you really review existing reports for free?+
Yes. Unique to our company, we peer review existing weathertightness or building reports free of charge and tell you whether further investigation is needed.
Which areas do you cover?+
We have offices in Ellerslie, Auckland and Te Miro, Waikato, and regularly inspect across Auckland, Hamilton, Waikato and Cambridge.
Are you insured and accredited?+
Yes. We carry full indemnity insurance with additional wet home cover, and our inspector is trained by the New Zealand Institute of Building Surveyors to their highest level.
